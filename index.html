    How to play

    1. Use Attack or Eraser on the right. Eraser can remove cells from any of your saved plans; two fingers move the map.

    2. Change brush size using the left slider; use Eraser for individual cells.

    3. Open Nations to select opponents, declare war, propose peace or offer an alliance. Tap an active zone to edit its plan.

    4. Squads spread along the planned front. Bright country-colored cells show troops. Small warm cells show workers; tap a base for its garrison. Ready sectors advance independently. The army assigns nearby troops automatically, keeps defensive reserves and requests support without returning home. Nearby soldiers defend the border; some stay in reserve. Recalled troops walk home before they can deploy again. Workers automatically use suitable conquered deposits. Choose a production priority in Match. Workers travel from cities, prepare sites and harvest. Cities add 24 workers per level. Strategic buildings wait for a worker to arrive. Workers lay roads for 1 wood per cell; roads speed up civilian and military travel. Each owned building adds 20% of its base cost to the next of the same type.

    Drag to pan and pinch to zoom outside paint mode. Tapping another human kingdom opens diplomacy. Victory requires 100% territory. No time limit.
